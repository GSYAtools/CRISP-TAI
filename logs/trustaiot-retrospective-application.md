# Retrospective Application Log — TrustAIoT

This record documents the **retrospective application** of the Lifecycle
Refinement Framework through its CRISP-TAI instantiation to the TrustAIoT case
examined in the associated research article.

TrustAIoT provides a lifecycle-oriented governance setting for AI-enabled
Internet of Things platforms, including documented lifecycle activities,
governance structures, process inputs and outputs, monitoring, feedback,
maintenance, change management, and policy-adaptation mechanisms.

This record reconstructs only those Contextual Refinement and Functional
Refinement relations supported by the analytical evaluation reported in the
associated article.

It does **not** imply that the Lifecycle Refinement Framework or CRISP-TAI was
prospectively executed during the original development of TrustAIoT and should
not be interpreted as independent empirical validation of the framework.

## 3. Reconstructed Functional Refinement

### FR.4 — Iterative Feedback Integration

The original TrustAIoT process documents monitoring results, testing and
validation evidence, user feedback, and subsequent change-management or
policy-adaptation activities.

This provides evidence for reconstructing an iterative evidence-to-decision
relation.

**Reconstructed feedback relation:**

**monitoring / validation / user evidence  
→ assessment against existing contextual and engineering records  
→ contextual or engineering reconsideration where justified  
→ subsequent lifecycle update where required**

The important property is not merely that feedback exists, but that evidence
can be related back to previously established trustworthiness-related
decisions and representations.

Where evidence challenges an existing contextual assumption, a prospective
framework execution could invoke **CR.5 — Context Revision Trigger**.

CR.5 is not independently claimed as part of the TrustAIoT rule reconstruction
reported in the associated article.

---

### FR.6 — Continuity Enforcement

The documented maintenance, monitoring, change-management, and
policy-adaptation mechanisms provide evidence for reconstructing continuity
requirements across platform evolution.

**Reconstructed Engineering Representations include:**

- Continued monitoring requirements
- Continued feedback and review obligations
- Maintenance and update requirements
- Preservation or reassignment of relevant responsibilities
- Review requirements associated with material contextual or policy change

These representations express **what must remain active or be reconsidered**
during continued operation.

The concrete monitoring, maintenance, change-management, and policy-adaptation
mechanisms documented in TrustAIoT belong to their subsequent process-specific
instantiation.

## 9. Relationship to contextual revision

TrustAIoT documents mechanisms through which changing operational, governance,
or policy conditions can lead to adaptation.

Within the Lifecycle Refinement Framework, evidence invalidating an existing
contextual assumption could provide input to
**CR.5 — Context Revision Trigger**.

The conditional relation is:

**new evidence or changed condition  
→ review of an existing CR.4 assumption  
→ CR.5 where contextual reconsideration is justified  
→ subsequent Functional Refinement  
→ updated Process Instantiation where required**

This relation helps explain the feedback architecture of the framework.

However, the canonical TrustAIoT reconstruction reported in the associated
article remains:

- **CR.4**
- **CR.6**
- **FR.4**
- **FR.6**

CR.5 should therefore be interpreted here as a conditional framework relation,
not as an additional case-level rule claim.
