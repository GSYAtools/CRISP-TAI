# Data Understanding — Activity Selection Trace

This document describes how trustworthiness-related activities were selected
for the **Data Understanding** phase during the construction of CRISP-TAI.

> **Interpretation note:** This record documents design provenance for the
> CRISP-TAI instantiation. The phase association is process-specific and does
> not imply that Contextual Refinement (CR) or Functional Refinement (FR)
> rules are intrinsically tied to this lifecycle phase.

## Rationale

Trustworthiness concerns considered during the construction of CRISP-TAI at
this phase include:

- Data provenance and lineage
- Assumptions about data quality and representativeness

## Selected activities

- Explicit documentation of data-related assumptions
- Early assessment of data governance constraints

## Traceability

These activities were selected in response to limitations identified in the
analysed CRISP-DM refinements, where data-related trustworthiness concerns
could remain implicit or be addressed only at later stages.

Within CRISP-TAI, their placement in Data Understanding represents a
process-specific instantiation decision. The underlying concerns may give rise
to contextual interpretations or process-independent engineering
representations before their subsequent lifecycle mapping.
