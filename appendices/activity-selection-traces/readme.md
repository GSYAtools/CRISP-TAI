# Activity Selection Trace — CRISP-TAI

This appendix preserves **design-provenance material** documenting how
trustworthiness-related activities were selected, adapted, and associated with
CRISP-DM lifecycle phases during the construction of CRISP-TAI.

The material originates from the detailed, dimension-by-dimension analysis
performed in the earlier CRISP-DM refinement study and is retained here as
**technical traceability material** for practitioners and reviewers.

The activity-selection traces should be interpreted as evidence concerning the
**process-specific construction of CRISP-TAI**, not as the definition of the
process-independent Lifecycle Refinement Framework.

In particular:

- The activities shown in these records are CRISP-DM-specific activities and
  candidate lifecycle artefacts considered during the construction of
  CRISP-TAI.
- Contextual Refinement (CR) and Functional Refinement (FR) remain
  process-independent mechanisms as defined in the associated revised article.
- Engineering Representations produced through FR are conceptually distinct
  from their subsequent placement as CRISP-DM activities or artefacts.
- References to CR or FR in the phase-level trace documents indicate
  **retrospective correspondence or support**, not that the corresponding rule
  is intrinsically attached to that CRISP-DM phase.
- The final process-specific mapping belongs to **Process Instantiation**.

The six phase-level records cover:

1. Business Understanding
2. Data Understanding
3. Data Preparation
4. Modeling
5. Evaluation
6. Continuous Deployment & Monitoring

The corresponding expanded figures are available under `/appendices/figures`.

This appendix does **not** introduce new requirements, refinement rules, or
validation claims. It complements the revised article by preserving the
rationale and provenance behind the first CRISP-TAI instantiation.

For the canonical CR and FR rule definitions, refer to the associated research
article. For reusable application artefacts, refer to `/templates`.
