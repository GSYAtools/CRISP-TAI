# Evaluation — Activity Selection Trace

This document describes how trustworthiness-related activities were selected
for the **Evaluation** phase during the construction of CRISP-TAI.

> **Interpretation note:** This record documents design provenance for the
> CRISP-TAI instantiation. The phase association is process-specific and does
> not imply that Contextual Refinement (CR) or Functional Refinement (FR)
> rules are intrinsically tied to this lifecycle phase.

## Rationale

Trustworthiness-related concerns considered during the construction of
CRISP-TAI at this phase include:

- Interpreting evaluation results in context
- Reviewing assumptions and priorities
- Identifying evidence that may require subsequent revision

## Selected activities

- Context-aware result interpretation
- Review of trustworthiness trade-offs

## Traceability

These activities were selected to support the interpretation of evaluation
evidence and its connection with subsequent review and revision.

In the revised framework, this design concern retrospectively corresponds to
the iterative feedback function addressed by **FR.4 — Iterative Feedback
Integration**. Its placement within Evaluation reflects the CRISP-TAI
instantiation rather than an intrinsic lifecycle location of FR.4.
