# Modeling — Activity Selection Trace

This document explains how trustworthiness-related activities were selected
for the **Modeling** phase during the construction of CRISP-TAI.

> **Interpretation note:** This record documents design provenance for the
> CRISP-TAI instantiation. The phase association is process-specific and does
> not imply that Contextual Refinement (CR) or Functional Refinement (FR)
> rules are intrinsically tied to this lifecycle phase.

## Rationale

Trustworthiness-related concerns considered during the construction of
CRISP-TAI at this phase include:

- Explainability
- Robustness
- Accountability

## Selected activities

- Justification of model choice
- Documentation of explainability assumptions

## Traceability

These activities were selected to connect model-level engineering decisions
with the trustworthiness-related governance requirements considered during the
construction of CRISP-TAI.

Within CRISP-TAI, their placement in Modeling represents a process-specific
instantiation decision. The underlying trustworthiness concerns may give rise
to contextual decisions and process-independent engineering representations
before their subsequent lifecycle mapping.
