# Deployment & Monitoring — Activity Selection Trace

This document explains how trustworthiness-related activities were selected
for the **Continuous Deployment & Monitoring** phase during the construction
of CRISP-TAI.

> **Interpretation note:** This record documents design provenance for the
> CRISP-TAI instantiation. The phase association is process-specific and does
> not imply that Contextual Refinement (CR) or Functional Refinement (FR)
> rules are intrinsically tied to this lifecycle phase.

## Rationale

Trustworthiness-related concerns considered during the construction of
CRISP-TAI at this phase are dynamic and operational, requiring continued
monitoring, feedback, and revision as the system and its context evolve.

## Selected activities

- Continuous monitoring of trustworthiness-related signals
- Explicit feedback and revision mechanisms

## Traceability

These activities were selected in response to limitations identified across
the analysed CRISP-DM refinements concerning continuity, monitoring, feedback,
and post-deployment revision.

Within CRISP-TAI, their placement in Continuous Deployment & Monitoring
represents a process-specific instantiation decision. In the revised
framework, the underlying concerns retrospectively correspond to functions
such as feedback integration, contextual revision, and continuity, while the
CR and FR mechanisms themselves remain process-independent.
