# Data Preparation — Activity Selection Trace

This document describes how trustworthiness-related activities were selected
for the **Data Preparation** phase during the construction of CRISP-TAI.

> **Interpretation note:** This record documents design provenance for the
> CRISP-TAI instantiation. The phase association is process-specific and does
> not imply that Contextual Refinement (CR) or Functional Refinement (FR)
> rules are intrinsically tied to this lifecycle phase.

## Rationale

At this phase, trustworthiness is affected by:

- Data transformations
- Bias mitigation strategies
- Privacy-preserving processing

## Selected activities

- Documentation of transformation rationale
- Traceability of inclusion/exclusion decisions

## Traceability

These activities were selected to ensure that preprocessing decisions remain
explicit, inspectable, and traceable within the CRISP-TAI instantiation.

In the revised framework, these design concerns retrospectively correspond to
the derivation and traceability of process-independent engineering
representations addressed by **FR.1 — Engineering Representation Derivation**
and **FR.2 — Decision Traceability**. Their placement within Data Preparation
reflects the subsequent CRISP-TAI process instantiation rather than an
intrinsic lifecycle location of FR.1 or FR.2.
