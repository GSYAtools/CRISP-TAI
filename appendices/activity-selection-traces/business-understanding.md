# Business Understanding — Activity Selection Trace

This document explains how trustworthiness-related activities were selected
and refined for the **Business Understanding** phase during the construction
of CRISP-TAI.

> **Interpretation note:** This record documents design provenance for the
> CRISP-TAI instantiation. The phase association is process-specific and does
> not imply that Contextual Refinement (CR) or Functional Refinement (FR)
> rules are intrinsically tied to this lifecycle phase.

## Rationale

Activities in this phase focus on:

- Early interpretation of legal and organisational constraints
- Explicit allocation of responsibilities
- Definition of trustworthiness priorities

In the revised framework, these design concerns retrospectively correspond to
contextual interpretation, responsibility allocation, and principle
prioritisation functions addressed by CR.1–CR.3. Their placement within
Business Understanding reflects the CRISP-TAI instantiation rather than an
intrinsic lifecycle location of these process-independent rules.

## Selected activities

- Context interpretation and scoping
- Stakeholder and responsibility identification
- Trustworthiness prioritisation and trade-off discussion

## Traceability

Each activity can be traced to gaps identified in the analysed CRISP-DM
refinements and to the design provenance of the CRISP-TAI instantiation.

Reusable structures for documenting the corresponding contextual refinement
outputs are provided under `/templates/contextual-refinement/`.
